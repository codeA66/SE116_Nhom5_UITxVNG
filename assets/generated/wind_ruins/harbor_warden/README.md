# Harbor Warden v2

Prototype model, created in live Blender through MCP.

- GLB: harbor_warden_v2.glb; units meters; Y-up; character faces +Z in Godot.
- Editable source: C:/Code/UITxVNG/blend_backup/harbor_warden_v2.blend (outside project).
- 24-bone rigid mechanical skeleton; 23 mesh objects; 16,465 triangles.
- Parts: head, chest plate, exposed core, fractured shoulder, limbs, mace, shield, two folding sail leaves and back flag.
- Sail_Open / Sail_Close / Core_Expose / Idle_Preview are demonstration clips, not a complete combat animation set.
- No AI, attack hitboxes, collision or boss encounter implemented. Not runtime-tested in Godot.
- GLB was imported back into Blender; skeleton, embedded materials and sail deformation checked.
- PNG previews use lightweight Blender Workbench material colors; actual Godot lighting may differ.
- The concept is an artistic reference. This prototype simplifies masonry damage and cloth detail.
