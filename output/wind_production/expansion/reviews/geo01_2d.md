# GEO01 — 2D review

Reviewer: Sol expansion reviewer
Result: PASS to model; no redraw requested.

The island reads as a distinct natural mass with a broad upper area and far-side rock ridge. It does not duplicate T01/T02.

Apply during modeling:
- Keep a real flat play area of at least 12 × 12 m and a clear route at least 4.5 m wide; place raised outcrops only on the far side from the gameplay camera.
- Define the walkable surface and pivot at local Godot Y=0; state that datum separately from any raised far-side outcrop. Preserve total bounds 24 × 30 × 20 m.
- Replace the overly sharp bottom point with 2–3 large, slightly offset broken faces. Keep the broad faceted stone and muted three-gray palette; do not add small surface noise.
- Use the actual 1.755 m fox and real 50-degree, FOV 30 game camera in the render check. The concept fox/camera are scale references only.

## Production direction update

The user wants the terrain to suggest the former Wind civilization's grandeur and craft. Keep GEO01 as a terrain module: provide a clearly flat reserved landing/build pad for future ceremonial foundations or stairs, with source-only locators; do not bake a shrine, landmark, banners, or emblem into the island. Broad crafted edge cuts and a few large terraces may suggest engineered stonework while preserving the open 12 × 12 m plaza and 4.5 m route.

## 3D preview feedback carried into v2

Sol reviewed an earlier GEO01 v2 preview and asked to connect the two narrow lower supports into broader, offset wedges. The lower terrain body was widened and blended into those base faces while preserving the approved plaza and broad-facet concept. The final preview is `assets/generated/wind_ruins/geo01/preview_v2.png`; a second reviewer pass on that final image was not recorded.
