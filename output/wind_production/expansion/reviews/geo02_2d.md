## Notes carried into GEO02 v2

- Keep a broad, flat central court and preserve a clear 4.5 m route.
- Keep the far-side outcrops as terrain; do not bake a landmark or banner into the island.
- Remove grass, moss, and noisy seam detail; use the three muted slate greys shared with GEO01.
- Shape the underside as two or three broad, blunt, offset broken faces rather than sharp teeth.
- Omit the small regular stair; keep only the large terrain terrace edge and a source-only foundation-pad locator for later modular architecture.
